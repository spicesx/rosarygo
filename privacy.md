# Privacy Policy for RosaryGo

**Effective Date: 17 August 2026**

RosaryGo is a prayer application provided by Michel Sfeir (“RosaryGo,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the RosaryGo application (the “App”).

## Privacy Summary

RosaryGo does not require you to create an account.

RosaryGo does not contain advertising, does not use advertising identifiers, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use general app-usage analytics or profiling tools.

Most RosaryGo information is stored locally on your device. If you choose to enable **Sync with iCloud**, selected app data is also stored in your private iCloud database so that it can be backed up and synchronized across your Apple devices.

RosaryGo also uses RevenueCat to manage App Store purchases and subscriptions.

## Information Stored on Your Device

RosaryGo stores information locally so that the App can remember your preferences and provide its features. This may include:

- Prayer and interface language;
- Selected rite;
- Appearance, layout, gesture, timing, haptic, and navigation preferences;
- Reminder and streak-protection settings;
- Selected and user-created themes;
- Rosary streak count and completion date;
- Calendar days on which RosaryGo recorded activity;
- Tutorial completion and progress;
- Notification read and deletion state;
- Widget display information; and
- Other settings necessary for the App to function.

This local information is not transmitted to us. It remains on your device unless you enable iCloud synchronization or use a feature that is provided by a third party, such as an App Store purchase.

Deleting the App normally removes its locally stored application data, subject to Apple’s device backup and operating-system behavior.

## Optional iCloud Synchronization

RosaryGo asks for your permission before accessing its private CloudKit database. No RosaryGo data is read from or written to CloudKit until you choose **Use iCloud**.

If you enable **Sync with iCloud**, RosaryGo may store and synchronize the following categories in the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account:

- App settings and preferences, including selected languages and rite;
- Reminder and streak-protection settings;
- Selected, imported, and user-created theme information;
- Streak count and the most recent completion date;
- Calendar days on which RosaryGo recorded activity;
- Tutorial completion and progress markers; and
- Read and deleted notification identifiers.

This information may reveal or relate to religious practices or preferences and is therefore treated as sensitive information.

RosaryGo uses this information only to provide backup, restoration, synchronization, personalization, and continuity across your devices. It is not used for advertising, behavioral analysis, or tracking.

The synchronized record is stored in your private iCloud database through Apple’s CloudKit service. It is not stored on servers operated by RosaryGo. Apple processes iCloud and CloudKit information according to [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

RosaryGo may use silent notifications delivered by Apple to learn that your private CloudKit data changed and should be synchronized. These notifications do not contain your RosaryGo backup data, do not display an alert, and are not sent through a RosaryGo-operated server.

### Your iCloud Choices

You can manage iCloud synchronization from RosaryGo’s Settings:

- Turning off **Sync with iCloud** stops CloudKit access on that installation. An existing private backup remains available to your other connected devices.
- **Sync Now** requests an immediate synchronization.
- **Delete Data from iCloud** deletes RosaryGo’s synchronized content and turns off synchronization across connected devices. Data already stored locally on individual devices remains on those devices.
- After cloud content is deleted, RosaryGo retains a minimal content-free marker in the private CloudKit database. This marker prevents another connected device from automatically recreating the deleted backup. It does not contain settings, themes, streaks, prayer activity, or tutorial data.
- If you later enable synchronization again, RosaryGo may create a new private backup from the information retained on that device.

## Reminders and Notifications

RosaryGo may request Apple’s notification permission so that it can provide prayer reminders and streak-protection reminders.

These reminders are scheduled locally on your device. RosaryGo does not send their contents, delivery status, or interaction history to us.

Reminder settings may be included in your private iCloud backup if you enable iCloud synchronization. Apple may process technical information necessary to provide notification and CloudKit services under its own privacy terms.

## App Store Purchases and RevenueCat

RosaryGo offers optional one-time support purchases and subscriptions through Apple’s App Store.

Apple processes the payment. RosaryGo does not receive or store your payment card number, bank details, or Apple Account credentials.

RosaryGo uses RevenueCat to:

- Display available purchase options;
- Validate purchases and subscriptions;
- Restore purchases;
- Determine whether a subscription or supporter entitlement is active;
- Prevent purchase fraud; and
- Maintain purchase-related history and analytics.

The RevenueCat SDK may create an anonymous App User ID and process limited technical and transaction information, such as device type, operating system, last-seen time, App Store receipt or transaction information, product identifiers, purchase history, subscription status, and entitlement status.

RosaryGo does not provide RevenueCat with your name, email address, contact information, advertising identifier, religious activity, streak history, themes, reminders, or iCloud backup contents. RosaryGo does not assign RevenueCat an account identifier connected to your real-world identity.

RevenueCat processes this information on our behalf to provide purchase-management services. RevenueCat may store and process information outside your country, including in the United States. For more information, see [RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

Purchase-related information is retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their respective policies and as necessary to provide purchase history, subscription management, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.
